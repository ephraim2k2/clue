POLICY SECTIONS (5)

01. What We Collect

We only collect the minimum data needed to run your account, track your streaks, and deliver your rewards:

Account Basics

Your username, email address, age tier, and encrypted password when you register a Clue account.

Gameplay & Streaks

Your 3-Life match scores, XP, 365-day streak progress, and Course → Topic → Test revision accuracy.

Reward Payout Info

Your mobile number and network (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) when you claim airtime, data, or cash prizes.

Device Diagnostics

Basic device type and connection latency to keep 5-second audio clips and live 1v1 duels lag-free.

02. How We Use Your Data

Your data powers your gameplay experience and prize fulfillment—nothing else:

  • Matchmaking & Leaderboards: Pairing you with opponents in 1v1 duels and ranking your scores in daily and national tournaments.
  • Adaptive Learning & Clues: Generating smart hints and highlighting weak topics in your curriculum practice tests without training external AI models on your personal data.
  • Instant Prize Delivery: Sending won airtime, data bundles, food vouchers, and cash payouts while blocking automated bots.

03. Schools, Live Parties & Clue Chat

How privacy works when you play with classmates, party guests, or community groups:

Student & Minor Safety

Academic scores are private by default. Junior profiles have restricted direct messaging and require parent or school approval.

Clue Party Guest Mode

Joining a live party via Room PIN only requires a temporary nickname—no permanent account needed.

Clue Chat & Groups

Group chats and 1v1 banter are protected by automated filters and two-tap player blocking/reporting.

Sponsored Challenges

Brand sponsors only see total participant counts—never your phone number or personal identity.

04. Data Security & Retention

We protect every session with modern encryption and automatic data cleanup:

  • End-to-End Protection: All traffic and reward API calls are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and stored at rest with AES-256.
  • Automatic Cleanup: Guest party sessions expire in 24 hours, custom party decks delete after 30 days, and deleted accounts are permanently erased.

05. Your Rights & One-Tap Deletion

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and GDPR, you have full control over your data at all times:

RIGHT 01

Access & Export

Download a copy of your profile, test scorecards, streak history, and reward logs anytime.

RIGHT 02

Update Details

Edit your username, school affiliation, phone number, or email directly in Profile Settings.

RIGHT 03

One-Tap Deletion

Delete your account anytime via Settings → Privacy → Delete Account or by emailing our DPO.

RIGHT 04

Opt Out Anytime

Toggle off promotional notifications, challenge alerts, or leaderboard visibility with one switch.
